Getting started

How do I register?

Sign in with Apple, continue with Google, or register with any email address. Email accounts must be verified through the link we send before you can see journeys or contacts. You must be 18 or older.

How do I find a companion?

Add your journey under My journeys: flight code, airline, from, to, date and time. Then open Discover to see travellers on the same flight, or on the same route and date. Safar Saathi does not sell tickets, seats or bookings.

Chat and WhatsApp

How do I chat with someone?

Send a connection request from their card. Once they accept, you can chat in the app under Messages.

Can I move to WhatsApp?

Only if you both agree. Each person chooses to share their phone number with that one connection. When both have shared, a “Continue on WhatsApp” button appears. You can stop sharing at any time.

Safety

  • Travel details are entered by users and are not verified. Use your own judgement.
  • Meet in public places, such as the airport check-in area, and tell someone you trust about your plans.
  • Never send money or share travel documents with someone you met in the app.
  • To report a message, press and hold it. To report or block a person, use the menu in their chat. Blocking removes the connection and its messages.

Your account and data

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, journeys, connections and messages. If you cannot open the app, email us from your registered address and we will delete it for you.
