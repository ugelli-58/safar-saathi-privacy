Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Version 1.0

In short

  • Safar Saathi helps you find and safely connect with people on a similar journey. It does not sell tickets or bookings.
  • We collect only what the app needs: your account, your profile, the journeys you publish, and your connections and chats.
  • Other travellers see your journey listing. They never see your email. They see your phone number only if you choose to share it with that one person.
  • We never ask for passport numbers, ticket or booking references, payment details or your location.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time from inside the app.

3. Data we do not collect

  • Passport numbers, visa details, ticket numbers or booking (PNR) references
  • Payment or card details
  • Your precise or approximate location (GPS)
  • Your device contacts, photos or files
  • Advertising identifiers, browsing history or analytics data

Please do not type passport, ticket or payment details into journeys or chats.

4. How we use your data

  • To run your account: registration, email verification, sign-in and password reset.
  • To match travellers: showing you people on the same flight, the same route and date, or nearby dates, ordered by shared languages.
  • To connect you: connection requests, in-app chat, and the WhatsApp handoff when you both choose it.
  • To keep people safe: enforcing blocks, reviewing reports and acting on misuse.
  • To record your consent and show the consent screen again if our commitments change.

We process your data on the basis of the consent you give in the app. You can withdraw it at any time (see section 10). We do not sell or rent your data, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects.

5. What other travellers can see

  • Any verified user can see your journey listing: your display name (or “Traveller” if you hide it), your languages, route, date, airline and whether you say the travel is booked.
  • After you accept a connection, that person can also see your flight number and departure time, and chat with you.
  • Your phone number is shown only to a person you have connected with, and only after you choose to share it with them. You can stop sharing at any time.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.

6. Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. They process data only to provide their service:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Authentication, verification emails and the Cloud Firestore database where app data is stored. Firebase privacy information.
  • Google, Apple and Yahoo sign-in (optional): if you choose one, that company confirms your identity and shares your name and email with us under its own privacy policy.

We may disclose data if required by law, or to protect the safety of users or the public.

7. WhatsApp

When both you and a connection have shared phone numbers, the app can open WhatsApp using WhatsApp’s official link. We do not send your chat history or any other data to WhatsApp. Once you are in WhatsApp, your conversation is covered by WhatsApp’s privacy policy, not this one.

8. Where your data is stored

App data is stored on Google Cloud servers in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google. By using the app you consent to this transfer outside India, in line with applicable law.

9. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, journeys, connections and messages: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Removing a connection, or blocking someone, deletes that chat for both people.
  • Reports you file: kept for up to 12 months for safety review, then deleted.
  • Deleted data is removed from our live database straight away. It may remain in Google’s backups for a short period before it is overwritten.

10. Your rights and choices

You can do most of this yourself in the app:

  • See and correct your profile under Profile.
  • Hide your name, or stop sharing your phone, in your privacy settings or in any chat.
  • Remove a journey under My journeys.
  • Withdraw consent under Profile → Withdraw consent. This stops phone sharing on every connection and signs you out.
  • Delete everything under Profile → Delete my account.

You can also ask us by email for a summary of your data, a correction, deletion, or to nominate someone to act for you, or raise a grievance (see section 15). We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India. If you live outside India, you may have similar rights under your local law, and you can contact us to use them.

11. Deleting your account

Open Profile → Delete my account and confirm. This permanently deletes your profile, journeys, connections, chat messages and blocks, then your login. For your security you must have signed in within the last 5 minutes. If you cannot open the app, email us from your registered address and we will delete your account within 30 days.

12. Security

Accounts need a verified email. Database rules let each person read and change only their own private data. Journeys are visible only to verified users. Chat is limited to the two people in an accepted connection. Phone numbers need separate, mutual consent. All traffic uses HTTPS. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and report anything suspicious.

13. Age requirement

Safar Saathi is for adults aged 18 and over, because it helps people connect with travellers they have not met. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

We will update the date and version at the top of this page when the policy changes. If a change affects how your data is used or shared, the app will ask for your consent again before you can continue.
